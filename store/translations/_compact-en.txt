The gods condemned Sisyphus to push a boulder up a mountain forever. At every summit Zeus sends it rolling back, and he walks down to begin again.

Sisyphus: Endless Climb turns the myth into a calm, hand-painted game about rhythm and patience.

• Push in rhythm: heave as the ring closes, find the flow, launch the stone over ledges.
• Mud, scree and wind: every hill fights back.
• Every climb at a different hour, from sunset to starry night. Seasons, rainbows and shooting stars.
• A living world on the far ridges: armies, Heracles and the lion, the Trojan horse, Atlas.
• A story in 150 scenes with Merope, Prometheus and Tantalus.
• The Book of Wisdom: 1000 sayings of the sages, a new one at every summit.
• Calm mode, 14 achievements, plays offline, 9 languages.

Free to play. Ads appear in the menus and between climbs.
