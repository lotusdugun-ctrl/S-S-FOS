REVIEW NOTES
Sisyphus is a single-player game that runs fully offline; no login is needed. Tap Start, then hold the joystick (bottom left) toward the hill to push the boulder; let go to rest. The Throw button launches it over steep ledges. The book icon (top right) opens the Book of Wisdom, a collection of quotes unlocked at each summit. Ads are served by Google AdMob in the menus and between climbs; the app shows no tracking (ATT) prompt.
